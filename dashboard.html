Valley Fever forecasts for California's ten highest-burden counties

Each county is forecast with news, Twitter and environmental data. Models are trained on a rolling 10-year window and forecast 1, 3 and 6 months ahead, then compared with a seasonal-naive baseline that repeats last year's value.

Counties ranked by total cases with best model and error by forecast horizon

How to read the errors. RMSE and MAE are averaged over every forecast month in every rolling window, on the log-change scale the project uses (change in log of cases per 100,000 from one month to the next). Smaller is better. The percentage shows improvement over the seasonal-naive baseline for the same horizon. Rate-scale errors (cases per 100,000) appear in each county's summary line, and the chart shows rates.

Features. Importance is averaged over every fitted window of the best model and grouped by variable (lags of one variable are summed). Media data are California-wide, so every county sees the same news and Twitter signal. Findings are predictive and associational, not causal.
